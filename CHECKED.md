## Not checked

- No person has checked the report, the test or the claims: the checks were made by models that did not write them, and, before release, by a quality pass (a Claude Opus session that did not write them, with OpenAI's gpt-6-luna as a second reader; its record: verification/quality-pass.md).
- The production counts (4 refused reads of 4; 52 skill reads; the reply on 2026-10-23) come from Alpha's private session log; they were counted by a script in Alpha's own repository and are not published, so they cannot be rechecked from here.
- The runs' transcripts are not published (they quote private prompt files); only their scores and tool names are. A reader can recount the table, not re-score a run.
- The 90,000-token setting was read in Alpha's configuration by the author; why it was chosen is not recorded where the author looked.
- Nobody listened to the voice: whisper heard it, which finds dropped and changed words, not a wrong stress or tone.
